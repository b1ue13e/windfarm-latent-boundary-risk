## 3. Core Software & Script Taxonomy

### 3.1 Data Ingress & Degradation Feed
- `windfarm_moe/arrival_feed.py`: Canonical `UnifiedArrivalDataset` and `DegradationSpec`. Enforces exact causal arrival symmetry: synchronously delays or masks `anchor_physics`, `x_hist` (stalled or shifted), `feature_mask_hist`, dynamic wake graphs, and extracts identical rule inputs.
- `windfarm_moe/data.py`: `CacheBundle`, `RegimeWindowDataset`, mmap loader for time-series arrays.

### 3.2 Model Architectures
- `windfarm_moe/model.py`: Directed diffusion GNN encoder + GRU temporal backbone; multi-task routing gate; dense point forecast head; auxiliary boundary heads.
- `scripts/train_capacity_matched_dense_wtb.py`: Unrouted dense counterpart with matched parameter count (~110k parameters).
- `scripts/train_and_eval_modular_mlps.py`: Decoupled modular architectures (`Cascaded Frozen MLP`, `Independent Consequence MLP`).
- `scripts/decisive_fair_risk_benchmark.py`: Direct conditional quantile baselines (`Missingness-Aware GBDT`, `SequenceGRUClassifier`, `QuantileResidualHead`).

### 3.3 Decision & Risk Evaluation
- `scripts/decisive_fair_risk_benchmark.py`: Evaluates 6 risk layers under unified causal ingress, single delivery moment accounting ($h=1$ and $h=6$), Newsvendor critical fractile $q^*=0.90$ ($\rho=10$), 5-bin validation calibration, and 24-hour day-block bootstrap.
- `scripts/eval_single_task_dense_phase_scan.py`: 30-condition grid evaluation ($\tau \in [0, 60]\text{ min}$, $h \in [1, 6]$, all channels vs no_pitch).
- `scripts/run_boundary_phase_scan.py`: Selective decision abstention benchmark vs random rejection and uniform margin expansion.

### 3.4 Verification & Audit Suite
- `scripts/verify_tste_number_consistency.py`: Asserts exact token presence of all 63 core empirical findings across manuscript, supplementary, and cover letter.
- `tests/test_verified_tables_consistency.py`: Automated pytest suite verifying Tables 1--4 against canonical CSVs.
- `scripts/compile_and_check_pages.py`: Compiles LaTeX and verifies 10.0-page budget compliance.
- `.agents/scripts/verify_gate.py`: Pre-completion reliability orchestrator gate.

## 5. Stale, Abandoned, or Contradictory Branches

1. **Abandoned P0-A (False Sensor Coverage Metric)**:
   - *Legacy Statement*: "Kelmarsh has 55.13% pitch coverage, Penmanshiel has 78.09%".
   - *Forensic Finding*: Script `rebuild_obs_windows.py` used `(physics[..., 1] != 0).mean()`. Pitch angle 0° is a normal valid operating state, not missing data. Real raw missingness is $< 2.7\%$ (Kelmarsh) and $< 0.9\%$ (Penmanshiel).
   - *Resolution*: Fully excised from manuscript and quarantined in `scripts/generate_quarantine_manifest.py`.

2. **Abandoned P0-B (Unshifted Historical Feeds)**:
   - *Legacy Statement*: Early 0.933 recall and 0.994 Markov recall.
   - *Forensic Finding*: Legacy `fair_degradation_replay.py` and `eval_markov_gilbert_telemetry.py` failed to delay historical slices `x_hist`, granting neural encoders an uncorrupted contemporary view at issue time $t$.
   - *Resolution*: Fully replaced by `UnifiedArrivalDataset` in `windfarm_moe/arrival_feed.py`. The real symmetric numbers ($0.416$ vs $0.196$ delay-6 recall, $0.962$ Markov recall) are universally reported.

3. **Isolated P0-C & Supplementary Table A11d Inconsistency**:
   - *Forensic Finding*: In `paper_tste_supplementary.md` Table A11d, modular baselines were listed with legacy unshuffled recall numbers (Cascaded MLP 0.785, Independent MLP 0.854, GBDT 0.868) while Joint Routed was listed as 0.416. This created a severe visual contradiction suggesting decoupled models performed twice as well under delay.
   - *Resolution*: Table A11d modular recall columns must be quarantined with `--` (or N/A) and annotated as non-lagged consequence models.

4. **Abandoned P0-E (GBP Cashflow Arbitrage)**:
   - *Legacy Statement*: "£442k / £3.12M annual operational cash savings under UK Elexon SBP prices".
   - *Forensic Finding*: Date mapping offset by up to 5 months for Penmanshiel; 24-horizon overlap double-counted energy; reserve $r$ was treated as costless short-covering without activation physics or market dispatch rules.
   - *Resolution*: Replaced by the mathematically sound Level-1 Newsvendor PSREI surrogate metric in engineering units (kWh/MWh) at $\rho=10$.

5. **Demoted Architecture Marketing (MoE Superposition)**:
   - *Legacy Narrative*: "Mixture-of-Experts routing is essential for wind turbine control-boundary modeling".
   - *Forensic Finding*: 5-seed paired tests confirm STGQ-Routed achieves statistical parity with unrouted dense heads ($p=0.380$ at $h=1$, 95% CI includes zero at $h=6$).
   - *Resolution*: MoE is strictly an ablation comparator; the primary scientific claim is anchored on latent operating-boundary representation and residual quantile calibration.
