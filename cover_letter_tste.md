We are pleased to submit our manuscript, **"Jointly-Learned Boundary-Risk Posterior for Wind Plant Reserve Pricing under Telemetry Degradation: A Defense-in-Depth Approach,"** for consideration as a Regular Paper in *IEEE Transactions on Sustainable Energy*.

**What the paper does.**
The manuscript targets a specific wind-farm operating failure mode: near the MPPT-to-pitch transition, the threshold label that identifies the active control law may be delayed, incomplete, or corrupted by uncalibrated channels and telemetry dropouts exactly when reserve screening needs an operating-state signal. We introduce a boundary-risk posterior learned jointly with the spatio-temporal power forecaster as an auditable defense-in-depth screening layer, and we localize where its value comes from with counterfactual controls. Three measured findings support the core mechanism: joint learning is what prices pre-dispatch imbalance risk and penalty avoidance margin (the joint posterior beats the global quantile by 0.57M, CI [-0.73M, -0.41M], and an independent GBDT posterior by 1.6M, while a continuous physical pitch quantile remains the honest clean-observation baseline at 15.538M versus 16.065M); the routing structure is what survives degradation (+0.737 recall gain under six-step confirming stream delay); and consequence signatures persist when defining channels are withheld (NMI 0.561, chance under permuted labels).

**Why it fits TSTE and its empirical pillars.**
The paper is written for a sustainable-energy audience because the technical contribution directly addresses grid-integration and wind-plant operational reliability. On the KDD Cup 2022 WTB benchmark, a five-seed training-split class-weight rerun preserves boundary alignment at NMI 0.721 while reaching RMSE 229.93, within 5.59 units of iTransformer (224.34), and the same train-only checkpoints are the single provenance-corrected source for all headline audits. The defense-in-depth value is established on three major empirical pillars:
1. **Plant-level PCC spatial smoothing:** Addressing the industrial reality that reserves clear at the Point of Common Coupling (PCC) rather than individual turbines ($P_{\mathrm{farm}} = \sum_{i=1}^{134} P_i$), joint posterior aggregate quantile pricing saves -11.22M kWh (95% bootstrap CI [-21.55M, -2.85M], strictly excluding zero) against the Global PCC Quantile and -14.94M kWh (CI [-27.06M, -6.23M]) against Gaussian parametric reserve sizing across the full operational envelope; in transitional regimes (10% to 90% pitching turbines), it saves -1.48M kWh (CI [-2.14M, -1.07M]) over continuous physical pitch rules, proving that boundary risk pricing survives fleet-wide spatial error cancellation.
2. **Industrial burst packet loss degradation:** Under an industrial two-state Markov-Gilbert burst packet-loss model ($p_{GB}=0.08, p_{BB}=0.75$, max lag 6 steps) with genuine input feature corruption across both wind-speed and pitch-angle telemetry streams, the stale threshold rule's recall drops to 0.840 (F1 0.904), whereas the jointly-learned posterior sustains 0.994 early-window recall and 0.967 F1 (seed-paired mean F1 gain +0.064, 95% bootstrap CI [+0.021, +0.087], strictly excluding zero).
3. **Decadal durability across 17.6 cumulative machine-operating years:** Across two independent commercial European wind farms spanning 17.6 cumulative machine-operating years under IEC 61400-12-1 density correction (Kelmarsh: 9 full years, 2016--2024, 6 turbines; Penmanshiel: 8.6 full years, 2016--2024, 15 turbines), Quantile Recalibration under Frozen Backbone eliminates concept drift, yielding 12 out of 13 rolling evaluation folds with cost savings ($p = 0.00171$ under exact binomial sign test; 10 mature folds strictly excluding zero in seed bootstrap intervals, with cumulative walk-forward pooled savings of -6.19M kWh on Kelmarsh and -4.34M kWh on Penmanshiel).

**Honest boundaries we foreground.**
We explicitly state the following boundaries so that reviewers can evaluate the manuscript on its intended operating contribution.

1. **Forecasting role.** iTransformer, Graph WaveNet, and other strong baselines remain better whole-sample forecasters; the posterior is used when an operator needs an accountable operating-state assignment in addition to an error forecast.
2. **Reserve-policy role.** The reserve analysis is a pre-dispatch imbalance risk screening and penalty avoidance margin proxy under validation-frozen empirical quantiles; it is not a dispatch optimizer, market-clearing model, or cross-farm deployment guarantee. With clean, complete observations, the continuous physical pitch quantile is the better pricing rule, and we disclose this as a baseline rather than a competitor. The learned strategies raise violation by about 0.001, which is disclosed.
3. **Mechanism attribution.** We do not claim the MoE routing structure creates the reserve increment. The matched modular benchmark establishes that joint learning prices the risk (joint routed 16.065M vs joint non-routed 16.076M; while cascaded two-stage MLP loses 0.347M and independent consequence models lose 1.28M--1.59M), and the routing structure delivers degradation robustness (0.933 versus 0.286 at a six-step delay). The MoE gate is one auditable implementation, not the claimed source of novelty. Furthermore, full-plant inference takes 4.12 ms (110k parameters) at the central substation supervisory terminal with annual electricity cost < £220 (< £1.63/turbine-year), precluding hardware cost inversion.
4. **Transfer role.** The routing mechanism recovers the declared boundary on a second independent anchor-observable farm (ENGIE La Haute Borne, five-seed canonical NMI 0.975), and the withheld-channel probe persists above chance on farms with partial pitch observability (Kelmarsh 55%: 0.340-0.378; Penmanshiel 78%: 0.302 with `signature_core` just below the 0.20 criterion). This is mechanism replication under a shared threshold definition, not automatic reserve transfer: cross-farm reserve use is still gated by a local evidence protocol, with the cross-farm held-out routing criterion reported per direction (aggregate pass 0.557; 0.341 in the reverse direction), and the sparse-farm pricing increment is not significant (-40k, CI [-202k, +129k]).
5. **Anchor role.** The fair-degradation audit shows availability value when the confirming stream degrades; the simple classifier control shows that clean-anchor standalone detection is not unique to the routed model. The anchor-stress, outcome-channel, route-evolution, and La Haute Borne replay audits support partial anchor robustness and anchor-observable replication, not anchor-free regime recovery.
6. **Dispatch role.** The reserve audit uses validation-frozen shortfall quantiles and rolling forecast-cell MWh-equivalent accounting as a pre-dispatch reserve/imbalance-risk screening proxy. The cost-scale markers are illustrative translations under declared reserve-carrying prices; they exclude optimal power flow, unit commitment, market clearing, delivery constraints, and settlement-price claims.
