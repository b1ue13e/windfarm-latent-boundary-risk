Dear Editor-in-Chief,

We are pleased to submit our manuscript, **"Jointly-Learned Boundary-Risk Posterior for Wind-Turbine Reserve Diagnosis at the MPPT-to-Pitch Boundary,"** for consideration as a Regular Paper in *IEEE Transactions on Sustainable Energy*.

**What the paper does.**
The manuscript targets a specific wind-farm operating failure mode: near the MPPT-to-pitch transition, the threshold label that identifies the active control law may be delayed, incomplete, or noisy exactly when reserve screening needs an operating-state signal. We introduce a boundary-risk posterior learned jointly with the power forecaster, and we localize where its value comes from with counterfactual controls. Three measured findings support the contribution. First, joint learning is what prices reserve risk: the joint posterior beats the global quantile by 0.57M (CI [-0.73M, -0.41M]) and an independently-trained GBDT posterior by 1.6M, while a continuous physical pitch quantile remains the honest clean-observation baseline (15.538M versus 16.065M). Second, the routing structure is what survives degradation: under a fair six-step confirming-stream delay the routed posterior keeps 0.933 early-window recall while the threshold rule falls to 0.196 and a jointly-trained but non-routed posterior head falls to 0.286. Third, the boundary stays identifiable when the label-defining wind-speed and pitch-angle channels are withheld (NMI 0.561, chance under permuted labels), and this signature persists across farms with 99%, 78% and 55% pitch coverage with negative controls at chance level everywhere.

**Why it fits TSTE.**
The paper is written for a sustainable-energy audience because the technical question is tied to wind-farm operation: can a forecaster expose the control-boundary state that concentrates reserve risk when the normal confirmation label is late or incomplete? On the KDD Cup 2022 WTB benchmark, a five-seed training-split class-weight rerun preserves the boundary alignment at NMI 0.721 while reaching RMSE 229.93, within 5.59 units of iTransformer (224.34), and the same train-only checkpoints are the single provenance-corrected source for all headline audits. At shortage-to-reserve cost ratio 10, the joint posterior's quintile bins lower same-router boundary-window shortage energy by 16.9% (CI [-0.976M, -0.300M]) and violation by 1.45 percentage points, with the parallel Physics-Aligned MoE family reproducing the direction (-4.26M, CI [-5.43M, -3.04M]); physical-bin baselines remain competitive, which we report rather than hide.

**Honest boundaries we foreground.**
We explicitly state the following boundaries so that reviewers can evaluate the manuscript on its intended operating contribution.

1. **Forecasting role.** iTransformer, Graph WaveNet, and other strong baselines remain better whole-sample forecasters; the posterior is used when an operator needs an accountable operating-state assignment in addition to an error forecast.
2. **Reserve-policy role.** The reserve analysis is a boundary-window screening proxy under validation-frozen empirical quantiles; it is not a dispatch optimizer, market-clearing model, or cross-farm deployment guarantee. With clean, complete observations, the continuous physical pitch quantile is the better pricing rule, and we disclose this as a baseline rather than a competitor. The learned strategies raise violation by about 0.001, which is disclosed.
3. **Mechanism attribution.** We do not claim the MoE routing structure creates the reserve increment. Joint learning prices the risk (the joint non-routed head prices at 16.076M, indistinguishable from the routed posterior at 16.065M), and the routing structure delivers degradation robustness (0.933 versus 0.286 at a six-step delay). The MoE gate is one auditable implementation, not the claimed source of novelty.
4. **Transfer role.** The routing mechanism recovers the declared boundary on a second independent anchor-observable farm (ENGIE La Haute Borne, five-seed canonical NMI 0.975), and the withheld-channel probe persists above chance on farms with partial pitch observability (Kelmarsh 55%: 0.340-0.378; Penmanshiel 78%: 0.302 with `signature_core` just below the 0.20 criterion). This is mechanism replication under a shared threshold definition, not automatic reserve transfer: cross-farm reserve use is still gated by a local evidence protocol, with the cross-farm held-out routing criterion reported per direction (aggregate pass 0.557; 0.341 in the reverse direction), and the sparse-farm pricing increment is not significant (-40k, CI [-202k, +129k]).
5. **Anchor role.** The fair-degradation audit shows availability value when the confirming stream degrades; the simple classifier control shows that clean-anchor standalone detection is not unique to the routed model. The anchor-stress, outcome-channel, route-evolution, and La Haute Borne replay audits support partial anchor robustness and anchor-observable replication, not anchor-free regime recovery.
6. **Dispatch role.** The reserve audit uses validation-frozen shortfall quantiles and rolling forecast-cell MWh-equivalent accounting as a pre-dispatch reserve/imbalance-risk screening proxy. The cost-scale markers are illustrative translations under declared reserve-carrying prices; they exclude optimal power flow, unit commitment, market clearing, delivery constraints, and settlement-price claims.
