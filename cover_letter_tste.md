Dear Editor-in-Chief,

We are pleased to submit our manuscript, **"Auditable SCADA-Anchored Routing for Wind-Turbine Reserve Diagnosis at the MPPT-to-Pitch Boundary,"** for consideration as a Regular Paper in *IEEE Transactions on Sustainable Energy*.

**What the paper does.**
The manuscript targets a specific wind-farm operating failure mode: near the MPPT-to-pitch transition, the threshold label that identifies the active control law may be delayed, incomplete, or noisy exactly when reserve screening needs an operating-state signal. We introduce a SCADA-anchored routing framework that turns the model gate into a graded boundary-risk instrument inside the forecaster. Three findings support the contribution. First, a signature-gate probe shows that the boundary remains identifiable (NMI 0.561) when the label-defining wind-speed and pitch-angle channels are withheld from the gate, collapsing to chance under permuted labels, and this signature replicates across farms with 99%, 78% and 55% pitch coverage with negative controls at chance level everywhere. Second, under fair input degradation where a six-step confirming-stream delay and sensor noise degrade both detectors identically, the gate keeps 0.933 early-window recall while the threshold rule falls to 0.196. Third, routing outputs alone---the pitch posterior and gate uncertainty---beat the global reserve quantile by 0.31-0.57M without any pre-defined physical bin. The clean-anchor rule and a simple classifier remain stronger detectors, so the claim is in-model route provenance and robustness, not standalone detection.

**Why it fits TSTE.**
The paper is written for a sustainable-energy audience because the technical question is tied to wind-farm operation: can a forecaster expose the control-boundary state that concentrates reserve risk when the normal confirmation label is late or incomplete? On the KDD Cup 2022 WTB benchmark, a five-seed training-split class-weight rerun preserves the routing claim at NMI 0.721 while reaching RMSE 229.93, within 5.59 units of iTransformer (224.34), the lowest-RMSE strict-cache forecasting baseline; the same train-only checkpoints are the single provenance-corrected source for all headline audits. At shortage-to-reserve cost ratio 10, gate-bin allocation lowers same-router boundary-window shortage energy by 16.9% (seed-paired CI [-0.976M, -0.300M]) and violation by 1.45 percentage points, with the parallel Physics-Aligned MoE family reproducing the direction (-4.26M, CI [-5.43M, -3.04M]); physical-bin baselines remain competitive, which we report rather than hide.

**Honest boundaries we foreground.**
We explicitly state the following boundaries so that reviewers can evaluate the manuscript on its intended operating contribution.

1. **Forecasting role.** iTransformer, Graph WaveNet, and other strong baselines remain better whole-sample forecasters; the router is used when an operator needs an accountable operating-state assignment in addition to an error forecast.
2. **Reserve-policy role.** Gate-conditioned binning improves a same-model boundary-window global reserve rule at moderate cost ratios with seed-paired support, while physical-bin quantile policies remain competitive and sometimes lower-cost; the learned uncertainty bins raise violation by about 0.001, which is disclosed. Supplementary Tables A11-A12 record the claim boundary and the bounded MWh-equivalent reserve-cost-scale translation.
3. **Transfer role.** The routing mechanism recovers the declared boundary on a second independent anchor-observable farm (ENGIE La Haute Borne, five-seed canonical NMI 0.975), and the withheld-channel probe persists above chance on farms with partial pitch observability (Kelmarsh 55%: 0.340-0.378; Penmanshiel 78%: 0.302 with `signature_core` just below the 0.20 criterion). This is mechanism replication under a shared threshold definition, not automatic reserve transfer: cross-farm reserve use is still gated by a local evidence protocol requiring sensor coverage, pitch or proxy observability, local boundary re-estimation, and a held-out routing pass, with the cross-farm held-out routing criterion reported per direction (aggregate pass 0.557; 0.341 in the reverse direction).
4. **Anchor role.** The fair-degradation audit shows availability value when the confirming stream degrades; the simple classifier control shows that clean-anchor standalone detection is not unique to the MoE gate. The anchor-stress, outcome-channel, route-evolution, and La Haute Borne replay audits support partial anchor robustness and anchor-observable replication, not anchor-free regime recovery.
5. **Dispatch role.** The reserve audit uses validation-frozen shortfall quantiles and rolling forecast-cell MWh-equivalent accounting as a pre-dispatch reserve/imbalance-risk screening proxy for balancing and reserve-review triage. The cost-scale markers are illustrative translations under declared reserve-carrying prices; they exclude optimal power flow, unit commitment, market clearing, delivery constraints, and settlement-price claims.
