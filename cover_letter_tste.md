Dear Editor-in-Chief,

We are pleased to submit our manuscript, **"SCADA-Anchored Regime-Aware Routing for Auditable Wind-Turbine Control-Boundary Forecasting,"** for consideration as a Regular Paper in *IEEE Transactions on Sustainable Energy*.

**What the paper does.**
The manuscript targets a specific wind-farm operating failure mode: near the MPPT-to-pitch transition, the threshold label that identifies the active control law may be delayed, incomplete, or noisy exactly when reserve screening needs an operating-state signal. We introduce a SCADA-anchored routing framework that turns the model gate into an auditable, issue-time control-boundary assignment inside the forecaster. A node-level mixture-of-experts gate is constrained by operating anchors so that each turbine-time route can be checked against a declared MPPT-to-pitch partition and then used when the corresponding threshold-label stream degrades. The contribution is a diagnostic layer for routed forecasting: it reports which operating state generated a local forecast, quantifies when that signal remains available under degraded labels, prices the associated RMSE trade-off, and states the checks required before cross-farm use.

**Why it fits TSTE.**
The paper is written for a sustainable-energy audience because the technical question is tied to wind-farm operation: can a forecaster expose the control-boundary state that concentrates reserve risk when the normal confirmation label is late or incomplete? On the KDD Cup 2022 WTB benchmark, the legacy strict-cache boundary-forced router recovers the declared MPPT-to-pitch partition (NMI about 0.87, ARI about 0.92), and a five-seed training-split class-weight rerun preserves the routing claim at NMI 0.721 and ARI 0.740. Because the route reads live SCADA anchors rather than waiting for a confirmed threshold stream, it retains 0.960 early pitch-window recall under a six-step label delay while the delayed threshold rule falls to 0.196; with only 50% label availability, the rule reaches 0.508. We also disclose the simple-control boundary: a validation-fit issue-time anchor classifier reaches 1.000 recall and 0.879 precision on clean anchors, so the claim is not superior standalone detection. A modular low-RMSE forecaster plus live-anchor classifier plus physical-bin reserve rule is therefore a legitimate alternative; our narrower claim is that the same saved forecasting model provides trainable route responsibility and route-conditioned reserve slicing under one provenance trail. The accuracy trade-off is measured rather than hidden: legacy RMSE 236.13 versus 224.34 for iTransformer, the lowest-RMSE strict-cache forecasting baseline, a 11.79-RMSE trade-off. The primary contribution we ask reviewers to assess is the auditable in-model routing layer and its bounded reserve-screening consequence.

**Honest boundaries we foreground.**
We explicitly state the following boundaries so that reviewers can evaluate the manuscript on its intended operating contribution.

1. **Forecasting role.** iTransformer, Graph WaveNet, and other strong baselines remain better whole-sample forecasters; the router is used when an operator needs an accountable operating-state assignment in addition to an error forecast.
2. **Reserve-policy role.** Gate-conditioned binning improves a same-model boundary-window global reserve rule at moderate cost ratios, while physical-bin quantile policies remain competitive and sometimes lower-cost. Supplementary Tables A11-A12 record the claim boundary and the bounded MWh-equivalent/EUR scenario translation.
3. **Transfer role.** The routing mechanism recovers the declared boundary on a second independent anchor-observable farm (ENGIE La Haute Borne, five-seed NMI 0.941), so the contribution is not tied to a single benchmark. Cross-farm *reserve* use is still gated: the Kelmarsh/Penmanshiel pair, which lacks pitch observability, does not authorize direct gate-bin reserve use and instead defines a deployment protocol requiring sensor coverage, pitch or proxy observability, local boundary re-estimation, and a held-out routing pass before reserve use.
4. **Anchor role.** The label-degradation audit shows availability value when an already defined threshold-label stream degrades; the simple classifier control shows that clean-anchor standalone detection is not unique to the MoE gate. The anchor-stress, outcome-channel, and La Haute Borne replay audits support partial anchor robustness and anchor-observable replication, not anchor-free regime recovery.
5. **Dispatch role.** The reserve audit uses validation-frozen shortfall quantiles and rolling forecast-cell MWh-equivalent accounting. The EUR values are scenario translations under declared reserve-carrying prices; they exclude optimal power flow, unit commitment, market clearing, delivery constraints, and settlement-price claims.
