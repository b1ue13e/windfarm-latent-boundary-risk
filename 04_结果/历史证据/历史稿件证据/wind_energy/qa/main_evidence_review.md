## Four-question and novelty judgement

**Why care?** The Introduction gives a defensible evaluation problem: upstream information can already contribute to downstream decisions, so additional inputs should be assessed against the decision objective and a common forecast. The Discussion also exposes the practical weakness rather than presupposing a paying interface: smaller learner width has not been converted to a measured operational benefit. Do not replace this honest conditional question with an unverified pitch-withholding or bandwidth crisis.

**What is new?** The draft's defensible empirical increment is the particular cross-year premium and severity trade-off, plus the fact that fixed-reference qualification and full-q finite-family identification return different calibrations. Current-power conditioning, dynamic reserve layers, decision-based input selection and near-optimal subsets have acknowledged precedents. The draft should keep its two concrete observed findings as the contribution; merely implementing an evidence taxonomy is not new scientific theory.

**Why believe it?** The core source chain is credible within its declared scope, and the corrected E1 chronology will make that chain accurately readable. The boundaries of the uncertainty construction remain material. Stored artifact verification establishes identity and arithmetic consistency, not 95% temporal coverage under arbitrary annual drift.

**Why Wind Energy?** The topic and quantitative consequences concern wind forecasting/operation readers, and the manuscript does not depend on a new AI architecture to justify that interest. Nonetheless, a reviewer can still regard one frozen interface/year as too narrow or question whether a +0.56% cost/+2.18% uncovered-amount trade-off resolves an important practical choice. Current results support an applied empirical article attempt, not a claim of novelty or acceptance assurance. The earlier practical-value stop decision remains compatible with reconstructing already completed work; it should not be silently overwritten by venue enthusiasm.
