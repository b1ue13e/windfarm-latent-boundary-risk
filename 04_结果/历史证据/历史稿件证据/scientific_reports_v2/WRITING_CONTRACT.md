## Fixed question and one contribution

After historical SCADA and a common point-forecast commitment, what proxy-cost and risk consequences follow from refreshing measured variables and using recovered-state features in the evaluated shortfall procedures?

The contribution is one controlled empirical comparison, supported by two separately reported development studies and outcome decomposition. It is not three independent method innovations, a new forecasting architecture, an identified physical mechanism or a deployment prescription. The measurement study is the principal reader route; the reconstruction study explains a distinct intervention. Calibration and C/R/U/V account for the limits to cost rankings.

Conclusion and abstract: written from stabilized results. No significant/robust/universal claims; no equivalence from crossing-zero bounds; no D-C inference; no new MSE interval, population mediation or pure information value.
