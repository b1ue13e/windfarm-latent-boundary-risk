# Scientific Reports story rewrite: pre-draft contract

One question: What does pitch add to shortfall decisions once direct power information is available?

One story: power updates yield the clearest cost improvement in the tested programs; recoverable pitch has a much smaller incremental cost effect; measured pitch can change procurement and shortfall even when total cost hardly changes. These are complementary observations rather than one causal mechanism or a proof of substitution.

Reader route: explain the measurement choice -> show the direct power-versus-pitch update comparison -> distinguish reconstructing pitch from improving decisions -> resolve the apparent cost/shortfall contradiction -> state the implication for evaluating telemetry.

Main text: scientific question, comparisons, results and interpretation. Methods retain the actual retrospective design, different masks/budgets, inference units, clock qualification and loss definition concisely. Public supplement retains full scientific results and negative controls. Internal issue IDs, source recovery, hashes, guard ledgers, validation counts, release history and execution-audit narration belong in author_working, not in the article.

Negative findings to retain: no consistent fresh-pitch cost improvement; unresolved score-control differences and one-day sensitivity; compression loss; higher reconstruction-study event frequency; estimator differences; failed procurement transfer and all three joint criteria unmet. Do not turn these into equivalence, universal pitch uselessness or unique allocation benefit. Do not hide the early historical clock exception or invent a correction.

No new experiment, calibration, resampling, measurement, threshold or original-result change. Preserve V1/V2. New title/abstract/introduction/results/discussion are authorised as a complete narrative rewrite, not a new scientific mainline.
